Example: Lisbon 4‑Week Remote Worker Plan

Neighbourhood Choice

Base: Arroios — safe, walkable, local, great value, with fast fibre WiFi and easy metro access to Baixa.

Daily Routine Snapshot

Morning: Deep work in a quiet café (e.g. Fabrica Coffee Roasters or Hello, Kristof).

Afternoon: Calls and collaborative work from a coworking space (e.g. Heden, Second Home, or Avila Spaces).

Evening: Fitness, sunset walks at miradouros, or riverside at Cais do Sodré.

Budget Snapshot

4 weeks in Lisbon on a lean but realistic budget:

  • Accommodation (private room in Arroios): ~£620
  • Food (mostly home‑cooked + a few meals out): ~£220
  • Coworking (part‑time/day passes): ~£120
  • Transport (Navegante pass): ~£40
  • Extras (coffee, activities, contingency): ~£250–300

Total: ~£1,730–£1,780 for 4 weeks — comfortably within a £1,800 budget.
