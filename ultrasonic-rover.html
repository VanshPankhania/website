How it works

  1. Sensors collect information about the environment.
  2. The Arduino processes the sensor data.
  3. The Arduino sends commands to the motor driver.
  4. The motor driver powers the motors to move the rover.
  5. The rover can avoid obstacles or follow programmed routes.

Sample data

Distance readings were collected at three servo angles: left (0°), front (90°) and right (180°). The data was used to see how the rover responds to obstacles and to refine its decision-making algorithm.
